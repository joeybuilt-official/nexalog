# Nexalog Brand Personality

## One-Sentence Personality

A quiet, deep library that breathes -- where captured thoughts settle into layers, form connections on their own, and surface exactly when needed.

## Reference Brands

1. **Obsidian** -- The graph-first, local-first ethos. Knowledge as a living network, not a filing cabinet. The density of interconnection without the visual noise.
2. **Are.na** -- Contemplative curation. Anti-algorithmic. Treats ideas as material worth sitting with, not content to consume. The unhurried pace.
3. **Letterboxd** -- Personal-feeling despite scale. Rich individual expression within a consistent system. Proof that "database app" can have soul.

## Anti-References

1. **Notion** -- Became a workplace productivity tool. Templates, team wikis, project boards. Nexalog is not a workspace. It is a mind.
2. **Evernote** -- Digital junk drawer. Capture-everything with no synthesis. Nexalog captures AND connects. Hoarding is not knowledge.
3. **Google Keep** -- Disposable sticky notes. Lightweight to the point of meaningless. Nexalog treats every entry as permanent, valuable, part of a whole.

## Surface Direction

**Deep dark, warm-toned.** Not the cold blue-black of Plexo's terminal. Think dark walnut, dark loam, the inside of a leather-bound book. A surface color in the family of `#1A1816` -- near-black with brown/amber undertones. Backgrounds should feel like depth, not void. Slightly warmer than true neutral.

Secondary surfaces step up with warmth, not brightness. Think aged paper behind dark wood. The palette should feel geological: layers you can see down into.

## Accent Direction

**Amber/copper family.** Warm metallics -- not Levio's gold (which is sunny, editorial, outward-facing) but deeper, more oxidized. Think `#D4854A` to `#C07040` range. Copper wire, autumn, the glow of a desk lamp. Warm enough to feel alive against dark surfaces, distinct enough from Levio's bright gold and Plexo's electric blue.

Secondary accent could pull toward a muted sage or moss green -- the color of old library bindings, connection lines in a graph. Never primary, always supporting.

## Distinctiveness vs Plexo

Both apps are dark. Both serve power users. The separation is fundamental:

| Dimension | Plexo | Nexalog |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Temperature** | Cold. Navy, electric blue. Machine precision. | Warm. Dark walnut, copper. Human depth. |
| **Metaphor** | Terminal, control room, infrastructure | Library, garden, underground network |
| **Motion** | Snappy, immediate, operational | Slow, deliberate, things emerging over time |
| **Density** | Sparse, functional, each element earns its space | Rich, layered, connections visible between elements |
| **Relationship to user** | Tool you command | Space you inhabit |
| **Text feel** | Monospace commands, status codes | Readable prose, marginalia, annotations |
| **Time orientation** | Right now (what's running, what's broken) | Accumulated (what you've thought, how it connects) |

Plexo is the engine room. Nexalog is the study above it. Same house, completely different rooms.

The family DNA (Geist headings, JetBrains Mono for code/stamps, 4px radius, bracket stamps, underscore wordmarks) keeps them recognizable as siblings. But walking from Plexo into Nexalog should feel like walking from a command center into a warmly lit room full of books and string connecting pins on a wall.

## Summary

Nexalog is the thinking app. Not the productivity app, not the note-taking app, not the wiki. It is where a mind extends itself. The brand should feel like that -- deep, warm, interconnected, patient. A place you want to sit in. Every surface says: your ideas have weight here, and they are not alone.
